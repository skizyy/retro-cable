Welcome to your digital cable lineupPick your videos and they become "live" channels — flip in and you land mid-show, just like real TV.
Import a lineup page — a page with channel headings and links under each (like the one your other bot makes).
In the browser use Ctrl+S (Save Page As) on the lineup page, then pick that file here.
Or copy the page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C) or a list of links and paste here:
What formats work?
  • Headings (or bold lines, or lines like Channel 5 - Cartoons / Cartoons:) name a channel; every link after it goes on that channel.
  • Direct video links (archive.org/download/…/file.mp4, or anything ending in .mp4 .webm .m4v .ogv .mov, or .mp3/.ogg audio) play live on the channel.
  • Archive.org /details/ pages are looked up automatically to find the playable file.
  • Other links (YouTube, other websites) appear as On Demand in the Guide and open in a new tab.
  • A heading called commercials makes those clips the ads between shows.
  • JSON also works: 
    {"channels":[{"name":"Cartoons","number":5,"items":[{"title":"Popeye for President","url":"https://archive.org/download/Popeye_forPresident/Popeye_forPresident_512kb.mp4"}]}]}

Setting up your channels

  • Make one main folder (e.g. My TV) with a subfolder per channel — the folder name becomes the channel name.
  • Loose videos in the main folder get spread across a few mix channels.
  • A subfolder named commercials adds 1–3 ads between shows on every channel.
  • Best formats: .mp4 (H.264) and .webm. Files the browser can't play are skipped and listed.
  • Local videos stay on your computer; pick the folder again each visit. Imported web lineups are remembered.
  • The built-in Halloween 2008 lineup is on by default; importing a new lineup replaces it, and "Clear saved lineup" brings it back.
